About VESPER

Not a boutique. A shelf, honestly labeled.

VESPER is an independent, frontend-only demonstration project. Every watch shown is a real, existing model — photographed by contributors to Wikimedia Commons under open licenses — but VESPER is not an authorized retailer of Rolex, Omega, Cartier, or any brand pictured here. Prices are approximate public reference points, not live retail quotes, and every checkout is a simulation: nothing is ever charged.
